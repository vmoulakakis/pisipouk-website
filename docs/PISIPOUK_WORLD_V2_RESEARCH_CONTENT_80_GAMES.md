## Correction to previous scope
- Age is the PRIMARY navigation dimension; 4 different age-specific home worlds, with Games | Drawings | Crafts | Stories | This Week under each.
- 20 individually playable editorially designed games per age = 80 games as acceptance requirement. **These are planned descriptions, not existing games.**
- Each game must have its own distinct goal, object actions, play feedback and progression; reuse of underlying runtime is permitted, repackaged quizzes are not.
- Target additional library: 20 original coloring/drawing templates per age (80), 10 age-tailored crafts per age (40), 10 original illustrated stories per age (40). Total planned: 240 categorized content items across formats (80+80+40+40); not 240 distinct engines.
- Age 2–3 uses short supervised sessions with sensory and cause/effect actions; older ages gain complex planning. Screen use never displaces physical play.
- Child screens are place- and age-first, NOT 16-domain navigation. Domains are searchable adult-facing metadata.

## Pedagogy and quality
- NAEYC: self-directed initiative, creativity, playful guided learning and accessibility; adult co-play for younger children.
- AAP January 2026: avoid prolonged engagement loops/dark patterns; high-quality content and time for physical play.
- Align to the four theme fields of the Greek preschool curriculum and map activity outcomes to curriculum metadata.
- Every activity includes objective, age, duration, one interaction contract, asset bundle, audio, offline continuation, safety and educator review.
- Demonstrate mastery without leaderboard, public ranking or shame; opt-in private stars for encouragement.

## Coloring / Drawing — target 20 distinct original A4 per age (80)
- 2–3: 20 high-contrast thick black outlines, one oversized motif per page, huge clear areas, no crowded scene. Finger painting or large crayons; also digital filling.
- 3–4: 20 simple story scenes (forest friends, garden, autumn, Greek seasons, bear's room) with 3–5 motifs; optional dotted pre-drawing lines and stickers.
- 4–5: 20 richer interconnected scene sheets with foreground/background, color experiments, open-ended extensions and basic pattern continuations.
- 5–6: 20 illustrated mini-story scenes, creative frames, mirrored patterns, classification and storytelling drawing prompts, some optional line tracing.
Each asset package: editable SVG source, printable outlined PDF A4, color example PNG/WebP, digital paint mask/regions, age objective, Greek short instruction, print proof and asset license provenance.
Use line widths appropriate to final print size and test child usability; avoid detailed narrow enclosed shapes for under-4s.
Print area A4 with ≥10mm outer quiet margin; proof fonts and contours at 100%; no watermark.

## Crafts — target 10 distinct complete patterns per age (40)
2–3 (adult pre-cuts): leaf collage, painted bear paws, fingerprint flowers, torn-paper rainbow, texture fish, paper sun, sponge cloud, giant apple, shape-house, animal handprint.
3–4: paper hedgehog, moving butterfly, leaf crown, bear puppet, olive branch collage, 3D tree, cardboard train, owl mask, rainbow mobile, autumn umbrella.
4–5: pop-up forest, shadow theater, accordion hedgehog, olive harvest basket, movable paper bear, windmill, paper lantern, seasonal diorama, 3D geometric house, rain shaker.
5–6: articulated character, mechanical wheel, working weather dial, standing paper village, multi-layer landscape, shadow projector, pop-up storybook, paper suspension bridge, seasonal calendar wheel, recycled animal habitat.
Every pattern MUST include material list, age, adult assistance, safety notice, ≥4 visually distinct step illustrations, photographed/illustrated completed result, 1:1 scale A4 vector pattern, actual PDF QA, and offline activity.
Crafts for ages 2–3: no cutting without adult, no small loose bits without supervision, non-toxic age-appropriate materials.
Each craft gets a new visual template; replacing a face on a generic cut-and-paste model does NOT count as distinct crafted activity.

## Stories — target 10 fully illustrated original stories per age (40)
- 2–3: 3–4 short illustrated scenes, repetitive familiar words, one safe interactive touch per page; co-reading.
- 3–4: 4–6 scenes, expressive cause–effect, simple prediction and one meaningful choice.
- 4–5: 6–8 scenes with beginning/middle/end, feelings, character problem and multiple choices.
- 5–6: 8–12 scenes, branching outcomes, phonological exercises and child story-author choices.
Each story requires consistent character model, original illustrations, editor-reviewed Greek text, professionally curated narration preferred, captions, sound-off fallback and parent print/PDF.

## Animation & Character Production — tool evaluation
1. Blender master 3D character/reference mesh and rig for the authentic Pisipouk (fur/stylized shaders, facial expression shape keys, skeleton, art-approved silhouette, texture atlases).
2. Rendered 2.5D scenes and spritesheets at efficient sizes for mobile; don't run a full WebGL 3D village on every phone.
3. Rive for touch-responsive UI, facial reactions, visual state machine and lightweight rigs; prototype and profile.
4. Spine 2D (licensed) for organic bone deformation, IK, inertia/secondary motion when imported raster cutout art is preferred.
5. PixiJS v8 WebGL for 2D game rendering, scene graph, collisions and touch, with carefully chosen physics simulation only when pedagogically useful.
6. Phaser optional for self-contained games with more complex scenes/physics; compare bundle/runtime against PixiJS before standardizing.
7. React/TanStack Router manages only app/navigation; stage changes should not reload page.
Motion art brief: wave hand, blink, breathing, anticipation, eye-follow, footfall walk cycles, object grab/release, flower bloom, water pour, paper folding, lightweight particle effects, interactive audio cues. Every animation has purposeful trigger and reduced-motion fallback. Never imply image-to-video clip is a stateful game.
Performance targets (proposal): 60fps high-end and aim at 30fps minimum mid-range mobile, route transition <400ms on warm cache, 2D atlas lazy loading, one active game scene and one mascot render; benchmark and adjust after actual device profiling.
