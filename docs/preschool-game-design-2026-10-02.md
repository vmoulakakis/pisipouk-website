# Pisipouk Preschool Game Galaxy — 2026-10-02

## Age 2–3
Focus: large tap targets, color/shape recognition, simple quantity, routines, matching and sorting.
Games: color baskets, shape matching, big/small, counting to 3, toy cleanup, animal memory.

## Age 4–5
Focus: patterns, sequence, emotions, classification, habitats, counting, rhythm and guided pretend play.
Games: garden patterns, emotion match, seed-to-flower sequence, habitat sorting, count planets, rhythm copy, animal memory.

## Age 5–6
Focus: phonological awareness, number sense, temporal order, problem solving, social decisions, working memory and construction logic.
Games: rocket build sequence, small additions, initial letter, rhymes, day sequence, kind-friend problem solving, space memory, rhythm pattern.

## Interaction principles
- Short 3–7 minute loops.
- Different mechanics: choice, sorting, sequence, pattern and memory.
- Immediate supportive feedback; no punishment loop.
- Every game includes an offline continuation.
- Content filtered by selected age and thematic world.
- Completion events are analytics-only and contain no child PII.

## Research grounding
Built around play-based early learning patterns from Khan Academy Kids and PBS KIDS, and developmental domains/milestones from CDC (2–5 years). Greek 4–6 preschool alignment remains consistent with the IEP remit for preschool curricula.
